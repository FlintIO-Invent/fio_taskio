# MotionMate Explainer

This document gives simple explanations for common MotionMate terms.

## Invoice

An **invoice** is a bill sent to a client for services provided.

It usually includes the service details, quantity, price, tax, total amount due, and invoice status. Invoices can also be downloaded as PDFs or emailed to the client.

## Client

A **client** is a customer or business contact that receives services.

Client records usually include names, contact details, company information, address, notes, and related work history.

## Service Request

A **service request** is a customer’s request for work to be done.

It is the “customer needs this service” record before or during fulfillment. A business can review it, match it to a client, schedule an appointment, or create an invoice from it.

## Appointment

An **appointment** is a scheduled visit, job, meeting, or service time.

It usually connects a client, service, staff member, date, time, and location. An appointment can be created manually or from a service request.

## Online Booking

An **online booking** lets customers request or schedule a service through a public booking page.

It helps customers choose a service and time without needing staff to enter the request manually. In MotionMate, online booking creates a request first so the business can review and schedule it.

## Dashboard

The **dashboard** is the main overview screen for the business.

It shows important activity, quick actions, and useful summaries so users can see what needs attention.

## Team Member

A **team member** is a user who belongs to a business workspace.

Team members can have different roles, such as owner, admin, staff, accountant, or viewer.

## Business Settings

**Business settings** are the workspace-level details and defaults for a business.

They can include business information, invoice settings, currency, tax rate, booking settings, and other preferences.

## Services

**Services** are the types of work the business offers.

Each service can have a name, description, price, category, and booking options. Services can be reused on invoices, appointments, service requests, and online booking.

## Availability

**Availability** controls when services or team members can be booked.

It helps decide which days and times are open for appointments or online bookings.

## Subscriptions

A **subscription** is the business’s MotionMate plan.

It controls which features are available and may set limits for things like invoices, appointments, public booking, services, or team access.
